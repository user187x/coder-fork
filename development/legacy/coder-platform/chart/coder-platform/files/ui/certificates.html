How it works

  • Every workspace you start gets your certificate as ~/.cert/<username>.p12, named after your Coder username and readable only by you (0600). Nothing else is placed in ~/.cert. Restart a running workspace to pick up a new or replaced certificate.
  • It is stored as your own Coder secret: only you can upload, replace or delete it, its contents can never be read back through Coder, and it is delivered only to workspaces you own. Every change is in Coder's audit log.
  • The optional password check happens in this page only. The password is never sent or stored: keep using it wherever the certificate is opened.
